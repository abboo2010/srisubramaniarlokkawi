Site CMS

Hero Banner

The big welcome section at the top of the home screen — background photo, eyebrow/title text, the three stat numbers, and the two call-to-action buttons.

The link boxes on the home screen that lead to About Temple, Deities, Pooja Timings, Sevas & Donations, Prayers & Registration, and Friday Annathanam.

About Temple

Vision, mission, history, and the temple's ongoing activities — shown on the About Temple screen.

Every person on the Temple Committee screen — President/Vice President, Secretary/Treasurer-style Officers, Committee Members, Internal Auditors, and Trustees. "Tier" decides where they appear on the page; "Sort Order" decides the order within that group. Auditors/Trustees only need a Name — the rest of the fields are for the other tiers.

Each shrine/deity card shown on the Deities screen — name, role, description, and photo, in all three languages.

Daily poojas ("Today" and "Daily" are usually identical — "Today" is what shows on the Home screen preview), plus the special Friday and Full Moon schedules.

Sevas & Donations offerings — name, price, description, and button text, shown on the Sevas & Donations screen.

News & Announcements shown on the home screen and the News screen. Untick "Published" to hide one without deleting it.

Add a Cover Photo so the tile looks good even before any photos are uploaded inside it — otherwise it shows the first photo found inside, or a plain color if it's still empty.

Folders live inside a Category — e.g. "Thaipusam 2026" inside "Festivals". Create the Category first, then add Folders under it here. Add a Cover Photo so the tile looks good even before any photos are uploaded inside it.

Photos shown on the Gallery screen, grouped into the Folder you pick below. Upload a photo, add a caption in each language — a smaller thumbnail is generated automatically alongside the full-size photo to keep the site fast. Use Bulk Upload to add many photos into one Folder at once.

Bulk Upload Photos

Choose the Folder, then pick one or more photos at once (hold Ctrl/Cmd, or Shift for a range, in the file picker). Each is resized and uploaded the same way as adding one photo, with no caption — add captions afterwards from the table below if you want them.

Membership records used by the Membership Status lookup on the site (visitors now search by Membership No., not NRIC — NRIC is kept here for your own records only and is never sent to the public lookup).

Bulk Import

Paste CSV with header Name,NRIC,Membership No.,Membership Type,Status (Membership Type must be Life or Ordinary; Status must be Active, Not Active, or Pending for Annual renewal — Status column is optional, defaults to Active). Existing members are matched and updated by NRIC; new NRICs are added.

Contact Us

Organisation details, address, phone/email/WhatsApp, social links, and the donation bank account — shown on the Contact Us screen.

Notice Ticker

The scrolling notice bar shown above the menu on every screen. Untick "Enabled" to hide it without losing the text. Malay/Tamil fall back to the English text on the live site if left blank.

Home Popup

A popup shown once to each visitor when they enter the site (after tapping "Enter"), which they can close to keep browsing. Untick "Enabled" to turn it off without losing the text. Title is optional — leave it blank to show a message-only popup. Malay/Tamil fall back to the English text on the live site if left blank.

WhatsApp Widget

A floating "Chat with Us" card shown in the bottom-left corner of every screen on the site. Untick "Enabled" to hide it without losing the text. It only actually appears once Enabled is ticked AND a Phone Number is filled in below — Photo, Heading and Description are all optional (the card can show with just the button). Malay/Tamil fall back to the English text on the live site if left blank.

The title and subtitle shown at the top of each screen (e.g. "Temple Management Committee" / "Meet the committee members serving..."). One row per screen — click Edit to change any screen's wording in English, Malay, or Tamil. Leaving Malay/Tamil blank falls back to the English text on the live site.

The text next to each icon in the side menu (e.g. "Temple Committee", "Pooja Timings"). One row per menu item — click Edit to change its wording in English, Malay, or Tamil. Leaving Malay/Tamil blank falls back to the English text on the live site.

Push Notifications

Send a notification to every visitor who has tapped the bell icon on the site to turn notifications on. There's no schedule — this sends immediately, to whoever is currently subscribed. Loading subscriber count…

Note: on iPhone/iPad, a visitor must first "Add to Home Screen" from Safari before the bell icon can turn notifications on — this is an Apple requirement, not something this app can change. Android phones/tablets and desktop browsers can turn notifications on directly from the site.

Admin Users

Create a login for a committee member and choose exactly what they can access — Content (this CMS), Prayers & Bookings, or both. Only a master account (like yours) can see this tab or manage other logins.
